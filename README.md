## Lab 0: Introduction to Unity

**Objective:** This introductory lab familiarizes students with Unity, a 3D game development platform. Students learn the basics of the interface, scene creation, and simple scripting to prepare for subsequent labs.

**Summary:** Students set up the Unity environment, explore its features, and create a simple scene. This foundational knowledge is essential for building simulations and interactive models in future labs.

## Lab 1: Obstacle Course

**Objective:** To understand the basics of object manipulation and collision detection in Unity.

**Summary:** Students design a simple obstacle course, implementing basic physics and collision detection. This lab emphasizes the importance of spatial reasoning and the fundamentals of Unity's physics engine.

## Lab 2: Self-Driving Car

**Objective:** To explore the concept of physical symbol systems and the limitations of pre-programmed instructions.

**Summary:** Students program a virtual car with a set of hardcoded instructions to navigate a course. This exercise highlights the challenges of inflexible programming and sets the stage for more adaptive systems in later labs.

## Lab 3: Fireflies

**Objective:** To study emergent behavior resulting from simple rules in a system.

**Summary:** Students simulate the synchronized flashing of fireflies by implementing simple interaction rules. This lab demonstrates how complex patterns can emerge from straightforward behaviors.

## Lab 4: Perceptron

**Objective:** To introduce the basics of neural networks through the implementation of a perceptron.

**Summary:** Students build a simple perceptron model to understand how machines can learn to classify inputs. This lab lays the groundwork for more complex neural network concepts.

## Lab 5: Connectionism

**Objective:** To delve deeper into neural networks and explore the principles of connectionism.

**Summary:** Building upon the perceptron, students implement a multi-layer neural network. This lab emphasizes the interconnectedness of nodes and the propagation of information through the network.

## Lab 6: Embodied Cognition

**Objective:** To understand the role of the body in cognitive processes through simulation.

**Summary:** Students create a simulation that demonstrates how physical embodiment can influence cognition. This lab highlights the interplay between an agent's physical form and its cognitive functions.

## Lab 7: Robot Tournament Part 1

**Objective:** To design and program a virtual robot capable of navigating a dynamic environment.

**Summary:** In the first part of the Robot Tournament series, students design a robot and program its basic navigation abilities. This lab focuses on sensor integration and movement algorithms.

## Lab 8: Robot Tournament Part 2

**Objective:** To enhance the robot's capabilities with adaptive behaviors and decision-making processes.

**Summary:** Building upon the previous lab, students implement adaptive algorithms, allowing the robot to respond to environmental changes. This includes obstacle avoidance and goal-directed behavior.

## Lab 9: Robot Tournament Part 3

**Objective:** To test the robot's performance in a competitive setting and evaluate its design.

**Summary:** In the final lab, students' robots compete in a simulated environment, navigating challenges and achieving objectives. This lab serves as a culmination of the skills and concepts learned throughout the course.
